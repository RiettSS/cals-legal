1. What Cals provides

Cals helps you record meals, nutrients, water, weight and fasting sessions. It can estimate a meal's ingredients, quantity and nutrition from a photo or description, transcribe a product label, look up catalog data and calculate nutrition for an entered quantity. Your local diary is not currently backed up or synchronized by a Cals cloud service.

The app needs an internet connection and functioning external services for remote AI and catalog features. Features can vary with device permissions, operating-system capabilities, language and availability.

2. Estimates are not medical advice

Cals is for general information and personal record keeping. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent disease and is not a replacement for a doctor, registered dietitian or measured food data. Calorie targets, portion estimates, fasting timers and nutrition outputs are approximate. Cals is for users aged 19 and older. The automatic calorie-goal calculation is also intended for adults aged 19 and older. There is no child mode or parental-consent flow in this release.

AI can misidentify ingredients, read a label incorrectly or mistake dry and cooked weight. A photo cannot reliably reveal hidden oil, sauces, recipe details or allergens. Check the label, ingredients and quantity, correct the result, and use a scale when accuracy matters. Do not rely on Cals for allergen safety, medication or insulin dosing, eating-disorder treatment, pregnancy needs, or a child's nutrition. Ask a qualified professional before making a health decision or starting fasting where a medical condition may make it unsuitable.
