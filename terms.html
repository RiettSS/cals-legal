These service terms supplement that license and do not replace it or reduce mandatory consumer rights. Our information-handling practices are in the Privacy Policy.

1. What Cals provides

Cals helps you record meals, nutrients, water, weight and fasting sessions. It can estimate a meal's ingredients, quantity and nutrition from a photo or description, transcribe a product label, look up catalog data and calculate nutrition for an entered quantity. Your local diary is not currently backed up or synchronized by a Cals cloud service.

The app needs an internet connection and functioning external services for remote AI and catalog features. Features can vary with device permissions, operating-system capabilities, language and availability.

2. Estimates are not medical advice

Cals is for general information and personal record keeping. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent disease and is not a replacement for a doctor, registered dietitian or measured food data. Calorie targets, portion estimates, fasting timers and nutrition outputs are approximate. The local food diary has no app-wide 19+ restriction. The current automated calorie-goal calculation is intended for adults aged 19 and older; that calculation limit is separate from access to the rest of the app. Remote AI and online features for minors require additional safeguards that are not finalized in this pre-release build, as explained in the children and teenagers section.

AI can misidentify ingredients, read a label incorrectly or mistake dry and cooked weight. A photo cannot reliably reveal hidden oil, sauces, recipe details or allergens. Check the label, ingredients and quantity, correct the result, and use a scale when accuracy matters. Do not rely on Cals for allergen safety, medication or insulin dosing, eating-disorder treatment, pregnancy needs, or a child's nutrition. Ask a qualified professional before making a health decision or starting fasting where a medical condition may make it unsuitable.

3. Your access and responsibilities

An installation may receive a pseudonymous identifier and credential without an email login. Protect your device and do not share credentials or impersonate another user. Losing the installation or its credential may affect access to server-side records. You can delete the server profile through Settings → Delete server profile. Cals does not automatically recreate a deleted profile; a new one requires your explicit choice.

Only submit photos, text and product information you are entitled to use. Do not upload other people's sensitive information without permission. Do not use the service for unlawful activity, bulk scraping, false catalog submissions, harassment, unauthorized resale, bypassing usage limits or damaging the service. We may restrict abusive access proportionately, subject to your legal rights.

4. Subscription and three-day trial

AI features require an active paid subscription or an eligible active trial. Pressing Add Meal without access opens the subscription screen. Creating an installation identity does not itself grant free AI analyses or start a trial.

The planned offer is an annual subscription with a three-day introductory free trial for eligible users. The actual local price, billing period and trial availability are shown in the app and Apple's purchase confirmation before you subscribe. Apple's eligibility rules apply; an offer is not guaranteed to every installation.

When real purchases become available, Apple charges the payment method associated with your Apple Account. Unless canceled, a trial converts to the displayed paid subscription and an active subscription automatically renews. Manage or cancel through iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Apple advises canceling at least 24 hours before a trial ends if you do not want it to renew. Deleting Cals or its data does not cancel an Apple subscription. See Apple's cancellation instructions.

Restoring purchases checks eligible purchases associated with your Apple Account. Refund requests are handled through Apple, including Report a Problem, and applicable law. We do not promise a refund outside Apple's process or a trial reset after reinstalling.

Subscriptions do not promise unlimited AI requests. The current default allowance is 50 AI analyses per subscribed or trial installation identity per day, resetting at midnight UTC. Unsubscribed identities have zero AI analyses. Additional per-minute and service-wide safety limits can apply; applicable limits and material changes should be disclosed in the app before purchase. Technical failures may prevent an analysis. A canceled subscription's remaining access depends on Apple's verified entitlement; a trial can end when canceled under Apple's rules.

Current testing status: no live Cals App Store subscription product has been created. Local StoreKit simulator transactions are not real payments and do not confer production access.

5. Your content and shared products

You retain your rights in the photos and text you submit. AI processing requires a separate explicit choice in the app; accepting these terms or subscribing does not replace that permission. You can withdraw the choice through Settings → AI data sharing → Withdraw consent to block new AI requests. Previously forwarded requests cannot be recalled. The processing is limited to delivering the requested feature, subject to the Privacy Policy. This is not permission to sell your private diary or to repurpose it for advertising.

Publishing a confirmed label to the shared catalog is optional. By choosing it, you permit us to store, display, distribute and correct the submitted factual product record so other users can look it up. The shared fields include the name, brand, barcode, preparation state and nutrition reference values, not your meal photo or consumed amount. Do not include personal information or content you lack permission to publish.

A shared record may already exist; submitting one does not guarantee it will replace an existing record. Community data can be incomplete or wrong. Email support to report a correction or removal request. Deleting the server profile removes contributions linked to that profile from the active shared catalog. A product snapshot already saved in somebody else's local diary may remain there. Removing only a local meal does not remove a shared contribution.

6. External services and food-data sources

Cals relies on Apple, OpenRouter, the selected OpenAI model service, Open Food Facts, USDA FoodData Central and hosting infrastructure for relevant features. Their outages, data changes and technical limits can affect the app. Third-party product names and source databases belong to their respective owners. External links are provided for convenience and do not imply affiliation or endorsement.

AI photo nutrition is an estimate; label-derived nutrition uses the values you confirm and scales them by the entered or estimated amount. A product's presence in a catalog does not guarantee a current recipe or a match to your actual package.

7. Local data and ending use

Check your entries before saving and protect any backups you rely on. We cannot recover a local diary that was never uploaded to us. Device backups are subject to your Apple settings. You may stop using the app and delete local records, withdraw AI permission or delete the server profile in Settings. Server-profile deletion preserves your local diary and removes linked active server records as explained in the Privacy Policy, with limited deletion-protection hashes retained for 30 days and aggregate/security counters for seven UTC dates. Hosting/proxy logs and providers have separate retention rules. Keychain data can survive uninstalling, and Apple billing must be canceled separately.

We may change or retire a service feature, or suspend access for material misuse or a security threat. We will handle material paid-service changes and any required remedies in accordance with applicable law and Apple's subscription rules.
